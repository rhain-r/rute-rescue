How it works

  • The Layers button on Safe Route and on the LGU Hazard Map opens this sheet.
  • Base map is one choice: Standard (OpenStreetMap), Topographic (OpenTopoMap) or Satellite.
  • Each NOAH flood horizon has its own switch, so 5-, 25- and 100-year extents can be compared.
  • Each overlay switch shows or hides that layer only.
  • Traffic shows "Live" from TomTom, or "Cached" with its age when offline.
  • Changes apply at once; Done closes the sheet and Reset restores the defaults.
  • Choices are remembered on the phone.

Figma to code

  • Sheet: bg_sheet top corners 30 dp, white, over a 24% navy scrim.
  • Rows: 56 dp, Label 13 sp + Caption 12 sp, dividers #E8EAED.
  • Switch on: rr_navy_light #24547F.
  • Icons: existing ic_layers_two, ic_center, ic_bridge, ic_car.
  • Satellite needs an imagery provider with free terms; this is flagged before coding.

How it works

  • The three routes are Lowest risk (recommended), Balanced and Most direct.
  • "Safest" is renamed "Lowest risk" because the app must never call a route safe.
  • Each card shows the time, a risk chip and one plain reason.
  • Tapping a card brings its line to the front; the others fade.
  • The drawer lists the chosen route's flood, bridge and traffic warnings.
  • A route with a high-hazard warning turns the button into "Start anyway".
  • The bottom navigation hides while routes are compared, so the map stays visible.

Figma to code

  • Cards reuse the 24 dp card look at 18 dp radius; the chosen card gets a 2 dp cyan border.
  • Risk chips reuse Badges tones: teal, orange, red.
  • Route lines: cyan #12B5D0, brand #24547F and grey #9AA6B2, with hazard segments in #E0483E.
  • Button: navy pill, 50 dp; "Start anyway" is the outline red variant.

3. Guidance with quiet rerouting

How it works

  • Guidance shows the next turn, time and distance left, and an End button.
  • The risk engine re-checks the remaining route in the background as traffic and closures change.
  • A lower-risk or faster path replaces the line without asking anything while moving.
  • A small toast says what changed and fades after 4 seconds.
  • Voice guidance stays out of scope, as today.

Figma to code

  • Turn card: navy surface, 24 dp radius, Micro + Section text in white.
  • Toast: glass pill with ic_check_circle in green.
  • Bottom card: the existing floating sheet in its compact state.

How it works

  • It opens from LGU Data → a center row → Manage capacity.
  • The gauge is green under 70%, amber from 70% to 90%, and red above 90%.
  • Quick buttons change the headcount by 1, 5 or 10, and the field sets an exact number.
  • Counts above 120% of capacity are blocked with a red message.
  • Status is suggested from the headcount (Near capacity from 70%, Full at 100%), and staff can override it.
  • Each save writes an audit record, and the grey line shows the last change.
  • Offline saves queue on the phone and show "Pending sync" until they upload.
  • Unverified locations show their landmark so residents can still find them.

Figma to code

  • Screen uses view_toolbar and the glass 24 dp cards from Data & Centers.
  • Headcount uses Display 28 sp; status chips reuse Badges.
  • Status pills reuse the list filter chip style (brand #24547F when selected).
  • Save bar matches the bottom navigation surface (glass, top shadow).
